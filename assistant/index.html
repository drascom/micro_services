Personal Assistant

Personal Assistant is a personal, locally running voice assistant. It runs on its owner's own computer or home server and is used only by its owner (drascom) with his own Google account. It is not offered as a service to other users.

With the owner's consent it can connect to his Gmail account to sort new inbox mail into priority groups (urgent, important, later, junk) by adding labels, marking messages as read and moving junk to Trash. It never sends email and never permanently deletes messages.
